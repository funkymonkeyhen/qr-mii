Welcome to the QR Mii Page!

Download QR Mii for your Wii: Download QR Mii

Instructions

Step 1

Download the latest release here

Step 2

Extract the ZIP file.

Step 3

Move the files in the apps folder to the apps folder on the root of your Wii's SD Card. Create one if there is none.

Step 4

Put the SD Card in your Wii and open the app in the Homebrew Channel.

Step 5

Select your Mii by pressing A on your Wiimote.

Step 6

Scan the QR Code with your NDS, 3DS, Wii U, or Switch 2 Camera.

Optional

If saving the QR Code doesn't work, just scan it with your phone and use your phone to save the QR Code.
